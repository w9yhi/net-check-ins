# net-check-ins

## The Logs of the ILYH Youth Net

The Illinois Young Hams' Youth Net began in the early days of the club, running on the Suburban Amateur Repeater Association's (SARA) repeaters.

After the club's (approximately) four-year hatius, the Youth Net returned under the club's new leadership, and is now hosted on the linked repeaters of the FM38 System.
Find out more at [`ilyh.club`](https://ilyh.club/net/).
